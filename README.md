Frequently Asked Questions

How do I switch between English and Spanish? Open the prayer you'd like to practice and use the language toggle to switch between English and Spanish versions.

How does Quiz Mode work? Quiz Mode tests how well you've memorized a prayer by hiding lines and asking you to recall them. Your results are tracked in your stats.

I found a bug — how do I report it? Email us at the address above with a description of what happened and, if possible, a screenshot. We'll get back to you as soon as we can.

Can I suggest a new feature or prayer to add? Yes! Send your suggestion to the email above — we're always looking to improve the app.
