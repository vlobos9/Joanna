# Privacy Policy — Level Up Catholic Prayer

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Level Up Catholic Prayer does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. The app has no network connectivity, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking of any kind.

Any progress, stats, or preferences you set within the app are stored only on your own device and are never sent anywhere.

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at [joanna.vlobos@icloud.com](mailto:joanna.vlobos@icloud.com).
